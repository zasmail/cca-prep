## Episode overview

Cat Wu, Head of Product for Claude Code, discusses how Anthropic's product org maintains
unusually fast shipping velocity, how the PM role is being reshaped by AI-native
development, and what she sees as the biggest under-used skill for builders working with
frontier models.

## Topics covered (per show notes)

1. **Shipping cadence compression** — Anthropic's product cycles reportedly went from
   months → weeks → days; the interview frames this as a structural shift in how AI
   product teams should operate, not just a speed increase.
2. **PM role evolution** — Wu argues the traditional product-manager playbook (heavy
   spec-writing, long discovery cycles) needs substantial rework in an AI-native org, since
   the cost of trying an idea has dropped sharply.
3. **"Build before the model is ready"** — a stated principle: ship products that don't
   fully work yet, positioned so the team benefits automatically as the underlying model's
   capabilities catch up to what the product needs.
4. **Underrated technique** — asking the model to introspect on its own mistakes/failure
   modes as an input to product iteration, which Wu flags as underused by most teams.
5. **Claude's personality as a product surface** — treating model personality/tone as a
   first-class product concern rather than an afterthought.
6. **Organizational alignment** — Wu attributes some of Anthropic's speed to mission
   alignment reducing the typical friction/consensus-building overhead of larger orgs.
7. **Operating principle: "just do things"** — described as the single most important
   heuristic for working inside an AI-native company, prioritizing action/shipping over
   extensive upfront planning.
