# Inside Anthropic's Bet on Claude Agents

**Publication:** Behind the Craft (Peter Yang's newsletter), creatoreconomy.so
**Date:** June 28, 2026
**Interviewer:** Peter Yang
**Interviewee:** Jess Yan, Product Lead at Anthropic

## Publicly Visible Preview Content

The visible introduction frames the episode around how Anthropic's own product teams are shifting from simple one-shot prompting toward building autonomous agents capable of working unattended overnight.

Two of five referenced "PM use cases" are visible in the free preview:

1. Understanding codebases by directly inspecting pull requests rather than relying on engineers to explain changes.
2. Synthesizing customer feedback pulled from multiple communication channels.

(Three further use cases are referenced by the page but their details sit behind the paywall and are not visible in the free preview.)

### Notable quote from the preview
> "Access to our codebase has been the biggest unlock for me."

This is attributed to Yan, describing how having agents review code directly — rather than depending solely on engineer explanations — changed her workflow as a PM.

### Episode topics listed (titles only, visible in preview/outline)
- AI agents operating autonomously
- Claude Managed Agents for extended, long-running tasks
- Building "analyst" agents (with a demo referenced)
- Debugging agent behavior through traces and self-grading via evals
- Processing large waitlists with agents
- Strategic focus on helping individual users first
