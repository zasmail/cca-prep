## Core claims

1. Shipping velocity in AI product teams has compressed from months → weeks → days as a structural shift, not just a speed increase.
2. Traditional PM playbooks (long discovery cycles, heavy spec-writing) require substantial rework for AI-native orgs because the cost of trying an idea has dropped sharply.
3. Products should launch before underlying models are fully capable, positioned so teams benefit automatically as model capability improves.
4. Asking models to introspect on their own mistakes and failure modes is a severely underused input to product iteration.
5. Claude's personality and tone should be treated as first-class product surfaces, not afterthoughts or branding concerns.
6. Organizational mission alignment directly reduces shipping friction and consensus-building overhead.
7. "Just do things" is the single most important heuristic for operating inside an AI-native company.
8. Action and shipping bias outweighs extensive upfront planning in fast-moving AI product environments.

## Patterns & frameworks

- **Build-before-ready positioning** — Ship products that don't yet work fully; architect them so they improve automatically as underlying models improve. Reduces risk of over-speccing.
- **Model introspection loops** — Query the model to self-analyze its own failure modes and errors; feed structured introspections back into product design cycles. Underexplored by most teams.
- **Personality-first product design** — Treat model tone, voice, and personality as engineered product constraints, not cosmetic post-hoc choices.
- **Mission-aligned shipping** — Organizational alignment on core mission reduces typical large-org friction points (consensus meetings, stakeholder alignment, spec reviews).

## Applied AI relevance

- **Bias toward shipping over planning** — Reduce spec weight; embrace rapid iteration. The cost of trying an idea is now low enough that experimentation beats prediction.
- **Model self-introspection is underexplored feedback** — Build loops where models analyze their own failures; feed structured introspections back into design. Most teams leave this capability unused.
- **Personality engineering matters** — Tone, voice, and behavioral consistency are product variables you control; don't leave them to chance.
- **Alignment multiplier** — Teams with strong mission alignment ship faster than larger orgs with more resources; prioritize recruiting and culture-fit over headcount.
