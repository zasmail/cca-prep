# Karpathy Mental Models

The canon of metaphors Karpathy uses to reason about LLMs — and why each one changes how you architect, evaluate, and staff an agentic system.

*Sources: the Dwarkesh interview ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md)), the Sequoia AI Ascent "vibe-to-agentic" fireside ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md)), the "Software Is Changing" / Software-3.0 and LLM-OS material in the X canon ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md)), the animals-vs-ghosts essay ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md)), and the LLM-wiki gist ([karpathy-llm-wiki](../research/notes/karpathy-llm-wiki.md)). The full talk transcripts (intro-to-llms, how-i-use-llms, state-of-gpt, deep-dive) remain IP-blocked; these recaps carry the load-bearing payload.*

## The thread

Karpathy's mental models all pull in one direction: **LLMs are a genuinely new substrate — not a faster human and not classical software — so you must build a new operating layer and a new engineering discipline around them, while staying honest that they are cognitively incomplete.** The unifying frame is the LLM as OS kernel: the model orchestrates code execution, I/O, memory, security, and internet access as a unified system, and the industry is stratifying around foundation models the way it once did around Windows/macOS/Linux ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md)). On top of that kernel sits **Software 3.0**, where the context window becomes "the main lever" for directing behavior, replacing hand-written code (1.0) and learned weights (2.0) ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md)). This is why context engineering is the primary control surface, not a nice-to-have — see [context-engineering](context-engineering.md).

The second load-bearing idea is that **capability is jagged and gated by verifiability, not by specifiability.** A model can refactor a 100k-line codebase yet tell you to walk to the car wash; performance is discontinuous, so the operative question before deploying is whether the task sits "on the model's rails" — verifiable, with training emphasis behind it ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md)). LLMs automate what can be *verified*, not merely what can be *specified* — a fundamental break from traditional engineering, and the reason eval design (see [evals](evals.md)) is the scarce skill. Coding led precisely because it is text-native, has a huge corpus, and ships with existing tooling — diffs, version control, IDEs — that make output cheap to verify ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md)).

Third — and this is where the full interview goes far beyond the essays — **the model is cognitively missing pieces, and naming them is a design tool.** Karpathy inventories the gaps against a brain: no hippocampus-equivalent (no persistent episodic memory across sessions), no sleep-like consolidation step, only limited sparse attention, no amygdala-like affective machinery, and a tendency to collapse to stereotyped outputs (ask for a joke, get one of three canned answers) ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md)). Each gap is a spec for external tooling: retrieval and logging stand in for episodic memory, a persistent wiki stands in for consolidation, and evals stand in for the missing internal error signal. This is the substrate under "decade of agents, not year of agents" — the missing pieces are why real autonomy arrives on an **autonomy slider** rather than a flip.

Fourth, Karpathy is a deliberate deflationist on timelines and mechanism. LLMs are **"ghosts"** — statistical distillations of human internet text — not **"animals"** shaped by evolution, and convergence between the two is not guaranteed (planes never became birds) ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md)). He is scathing about how the models are *trained*: **"reinforcement learning is terrible."** Credit assignment broadcasts a single scalar reward uniformly across an entire successful trajectory — "sucking supervision through a straw" — upweighting even the wrong turns that happened to precede the right answer, and process-level supervision is gameable because LLM judges fall to adversarial token sequences ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md)). Meanwhile **in-context learning** — gradient-descent-like adaptation via attention — is the single most impressive capability, because anything in the window is *directly accessible* while anything in the weights is "hazy recollection."

Finally, the human role narrows but doesn't vanish: **taste, judgment, decomposition, spec-writing, diff-review, and eval design are the durable skills.** Agents "outsource thinking but not understanding," and understanding remains the bottleneck for directing them ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md)). His persistent LLM-wiki — an agent-maintained knowledge base that compounds synthesis over time via an ingest-query-lint loop — is the concrete artifact of that discipline, and a direct answer to the missing-consolidation gap ([karpathy-llm-wiki](../research/notes/karpathy-llm-wiki.md)); see [memory-and-compounding](memory-and-compounding.md).

## Patterns

**LLM-as-OS kernel.** Treat the model as the kernel process orchestrating modalities, code, memory, and security — not a chatbot. *Use when* designing agent infrastructure: computing concepts (attack surface, permissioning, assembly-level traces, defaults + app store) carry over directly. ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md))

**Software 3.0 — context window as control surface.** Direct behavior by curating the window rather than writing code or retraining weights. *Use when* choosing between prompting, RAG, and fine-tuning: reach for context first, because in-context access beats weight recall. ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md), [dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**Jagged intelligence / verifiability partition.** Before deploying, diagnose whether the task has an automatic reward signal. *Use when* scoping a wedge: coding, math, and test-gated domains improve fastest; unverifiable domains stall (the MenuGen Stripe/Google-account bug is syntactically correct but semantically wrong precisely because it wasn't verified). ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md))

**Vibe coding vs. agentic engineering.** Vibe coding raises the *floor* (accessibility for casual users); agentic engineering raises the *ceiling* and demands specs, oversight, security testing, and permissioning. *Use when* setting team process — don't ship production on vibes. Connects to [claude-code-workflows](claude-code-workflows.md) and [enforcement-reliability](enforcement-reliability.md). ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md), [karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md))

**Animals vs. ghosts.** Model LLMs as human-text distillations, not creatures with intrinsic agency; pretraining is "our crappy evolution" — a finite-data substitute for the billions of evolved parameters an animal is born with. *Use when* tempted to reason about model "wants" — test behavior instead. ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md))

**Cognitive-gaps checklist.** Audit any agent design against the five missing pieces — episodic memory, consolidation, sparse attention, affect, output diversity — and name which external system compensates for each. *Use when* deciding what tooling an agent actually needs; the gap *is* the requirement. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**In-context learning as working memory.** Anything in the window is directly accessible; anything in the weights is hazy recollection. *Use when* trading off retrieval vs. fine-tuning — put the load-bearing facts in context, not in a training run. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**Cognitive core + external memory.** Separate a small reasoning core from bulk factual memory held in external retrieval — a ~billion-parameter core stripped of trivia could match far larger models on reasoning, with the caveat "you can't think if you're looking things up constantly." *Use when* deciding scale vs. retrieval tradeoffs. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**Autonomy slider.** Ramp agents' share of work volume while humans supervise small teams of agents, rather than flipping to full autonomy. The gradient tracks verifiability: call centers (repetitive, digital, bounded) automate fast; radiology (messier than pure image classification) resists despite excellent vision models. *Use when* setting deployment guardrails, staffing, and which workflows to attack first. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**Agent-maintained wiki (ingest-query-lint).** An LLM-generated markdown knowledge layer over immutable sources, kept consistent by linting for contradictions/orphans — knowledge that compounds instead of re-deriving per query, and the concrete fix for the missing-consolidation gap. *Use when* building durable agent memory. ([karpathy-llm-wiki](../research/notes/karpathy-llm-wiki.md))

## Numbers & rules of thumb

- **December 2025 = the agentic inflection.** Coding "basically didn't work before December"; workflow flipped from ~80% manual / 20% agents to ~80% agents / 20% human edits in weeks. ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md), [karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md))
- **~10 tokens/sec single-threaded execution** — the throughput bottleneck for agentic loops. ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md))
- **Decade to reliable workplace agents**, drawn from 15 years of watching field predictions — not "year of agents." ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
- **~2% annual GDP growth for ~250 years** — AGI shows up as continuation of the trend, not a visible discontinuity (you can't find the 2008 iPhone launch in GDP data either). ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
- **~Billion-parameter cognitive core** could match far larger models on reasoning if stripped of trivia and paired with retrieval; scaling-down has run ~two orders of magnitude in two years. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
- **Three eras of deep learning:** task-specific nets (post-AlexNet, ~2012) → premature agents (2013–2015, Atari RL / OpenAI Universe) → LLMs / representation learning (today's agents sit on the pretraining foundation). ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
- **Five cognitive gaps:** no persistent episodic memory, no sleep-like consolidation, limited sparse attention, no affect machinery, collapse to stereotyped outputs (≈3 canned jokes). ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
- **100k-line refactor** as the jagged-capability high-water mark, next to trivial commonsense failures. ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md))
- **"Double-digit percent uncertainty"** — Karpathy's own confidence caveat on the entire animals-vs-ghosts thesis. ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md))

## Where speakers disagree

**Karpathy vs. the "year of agents" consensus.** Karpathy explicitly deflates the near-term hype — a decade, an autonomy slider, slow diffusion ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md)) — while simultaneously insisting the December 2025 coding break was *discontinuous*, "not gradually and over time" ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md)). The tension is real and productive: a sharp capability jump in one verifiable domain (code) is compatible with a slow economy-wide rollout, because most work is not yet verifiable.

**Karpathy vs. Sutton (bitter lesson / child machines).** Sutton frames intelligence as RL from a blank-slate child machine; Karpathy counters that animal brains ship with billions of evolved parameters and that pretraining is the ghost's substitute for that innate initialization — pure RL without inductive priors is "theoretically elegant but practically insufficient." He also argues the bitter lesson may not fully apply to LLMs given their dependence on finite human data. ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md))

**Karpathy vs. the RL-maximalist / fast-takeoff camp.** He calls RL as practiced "terrible" — credit assignment "sucks supervision through a straw," and process-level supervision is gameable by adversarial token sequences that fool LLM judges into perfect scores. Because he doubts current models can autonomously drive novel research architecture (his nanochat experience: models impose conventions, over-engineer, suggest deprecated APIs, add boilerplate — autocomplete often beat agentic coding), he rejects the prerequisite many assume for a fast intelligence explosion. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

**Karpathy vs. himself on scaling-down.** Dwarkesh pushes that empirical trends (two orders of magnitude in two years) imply the cognitive core could shrink further; Karpathy agrees in spirit but insists on a baseline — "you can't think if you're looking things up constantly." ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

## Interview-ready takes

1. **"Verifiability, not specifiability, is the deployment gate."** The right first question for any agent project is whether the task has an automatic reward signal — that's what determines whether it sits on the model's rails, why coding led (text-native + diffs + version control make output cheap to verify), and why call centers automate before radiology. ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md), [dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

2. **"Vibe coding raises the floor; agentic engineering raises the ceiling — and only the second one ships to production."** Production demands specs, diff-review, eval design, and *programmatic* guardrails (hooks, not prompts), which ties directly to [enforcement-reliability](enforcement-reliability.md). ([karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md))

3. **"Design against the cognitive gaps, don't wish them away."** Name which of the five missing pieces each part of your system compensates for — retrieval and logs for episodic memory, a persistent wiki for consolidation, evals for the missing internal error signal. It's a concrete architecture checklist, not philosophy. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

4. **"Treat the model as a ghost, not an animal — test behavior, don't reason about intent."** Anti-anthropomorphism is a design principle: it favors empirical evals over narratives about what the model "wants," and it explains why he distrusts gameable process-supervision judges. ([karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts](../research/notes/karpathy-animals-vs-ghosts.md), [dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

5. **"Put the load-bearing facts in context, not in a fine-tune."** In-context learning is the model's most impressive capability — direct access beats hazy weight recall — so context engineering and retrieval outrank retraining for most applied problems. ([dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))

6. **"The durable human skills are decomposition, spec-writing, and eval design."** Agents outsource thinking but not understanding; the leverage moved from writing code to decomposing work so agents execute cleanly and catch errors before they compound. ([karpathy-x-posts](../research/notes/karpathy-x-posts.md), [karpathy-sequoia-recap](../research/notes/karpathy-sequoia-recap.md))

7. **"Knowledge should compound, not re-derive."** A persistent agent-maintained wiki (ingest-query-lint) beats stateless RAG because bookkeeping — not reading or thinking — is the real cost of knowledge work; this is the antidote to LLMs' missing episodic memory and consolidation. ([karpathy-llm-wiki](../research/notes/karpathy-llm-wiki.md), [dwarkesh-karpathy](../research/notes/dwarkesh-karpathy.md))
